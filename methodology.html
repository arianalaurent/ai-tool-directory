Methodology

Picks are ranked per use case by our editorial team plus an AI agent that scans X and launch sites for new releases.

  • Fit for the use case: how well it does the specific job
  • Output quality
  • Ease of use
  • Value and free option
  • Momentum: updates and community traction

Verdict scores out of 10 are editorial ratings, not lab benchmarks. We never state prices we have not verified; when unsure we say "see site". Sponsored listings are always labeled and never change rankings.
